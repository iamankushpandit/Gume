Why every card carries a screenshot, a date and a checksum. Pages move, paywall, get edited or disappear, and an article that cannot be shown is a claim rather than a fact. So each entry here records the date the piece was published, a screenshot of the page taken on a stated date, the SHA-256 of that image so a swapped file is detectable, and a copy in the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine where one could be made. The screenshots are served from this site and never from the outlet, so they stay up when the original does not. The data behind this page is site/featured.json; the images are in site/featured/.
